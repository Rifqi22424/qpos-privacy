This Privacy Policy explains what information the QPOS: Free & Offline Cashier mobile application (the "App", package name com.pemimpinteknologi.qpos) keeps on your device, what it sends to us, why, and the choices you have.

In short
  • Your business records are stored on your device and the App works offline.
  • For everyone, the App sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports so we can fix problems and improve the App. These do not include your customers' names or the names of the items you sell.
  • Only if you agree on the "Profil toko" screen, the App also sends your store profile and store data (menu, customers' names, orders and cash entries) to our database in Jakarta, Indonesia. You can stop at any time and the data is deleted.
  • We do not sell your data.

1. Who we are

The App is provided by Rhani Sri Ervina, trading as Pemimpin Teknologi Indonesia, Puri Cibereum 1, Jl. Gunung Singgalang Blok I No. 02, Sukabumi 43165, Indonesia ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: rsriervina@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

The App stores what you enter in a database on your device: business details (store name, address, phone, logo), menu items and categories, prices and stock, customers you save, orders and payments, cash entries, settings and backups you create. This data stays on your device unless you choose to share it (section 4), export it, or back it up yourself.

3. Data sent for all users

a. Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the App is used, it sends events such as screens opened and features used (for example: an order was completed, a receipt was printed, a report was exported). Some events include a number or type, such as an order's total amount, the number of items in it and the payment method type. It also sends device information (model, operating-system version, app version, language), an approximate location derived by Google from your IP address, and settings such as whether you use the free or Pro version, your business type and size ranges (for example, number of menu items). Usage statistics do not include your customers' names, item names or your business name.

b. Crash and error reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the App crashes or a feature fails, it sends a technical report: the error, the device state, the App version, and the last screens and actions before the problem. Reports are only sent from released versions of the App.

c. Device identifier

Usage statistics, crash reports and shared store data are linked to a device identifier: a one-way hashed value derived from your device information. It does not reveal your phone number, name or e-mail. We use it to connect a crash report or a support request to the right device, and to recognise a Pro license on your device. Firebase also creates its own random app-instance identifiers.

d. License activation

When you activate or deactivate a Pro license, the App sends your serial key, the device identifier and, for deactivation, the details you enter to receive a one-time code, to our activation server.

e. Support contact

The App downloads our current WhatsApp support number from a configuration file. This request contains no personal data beyond what any internet request contains (such as your IP address).

4. Store data you choose to share

When your store has been using the App for a while, the App asks you to complete your store profile on the "Profil toko" screen. Filling in the profile is required, but sharing is optional: data is only sent if you tick "Bantu kembangkan QPOS dengan berbagi data toko". If you don't tick it, your profile is saved on your device only.

If you agree, the App sends:

WhatDetails
OwnerYour name and WhatsApp number.
BusinessStore name, address, phone and business type.
MenuMenu items and categories, with prices, cost prices, stock and barcodes.

Why: to understand how stores use QPOS, improve the App, help you when you contact us, and contact you about QPOS (for example, for feedback or offers relevant to your store). We do not use your customers' names to contact them.

Legal basis: your consent. Before sharing your customers' names, make sure you are allowed to do so.

Where: Google Cloud Firestore (Firebase), in the asia-southeast2 region (Jakarta, Indonesia). Each device can only write its own data; only we can view it.

How often: the App sends changes about every 30 minutes and when you close it; if you are offline, it sends them later. When you first agree, the last 90 days of records are sent.

Stopping: open Pengaturan (Settings) → Profil toko and tap "Berhenti berbagi & hapus data saya", or untick the box and save. Sharing stops and the data already sent is deleted. If you uninstall the App without stopping first, contact us and we will delete it.

5. How we use information

  • To run the App's features and activate Pro licenses.
  • To find and fix crashes and errors.
  • To understand which features are used and improve the App.
  • If you agreed to share store data: to analyse how stores use the App, support you, and contact you about QPOS.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

6. Who we share it with

  • Google (Firebase) processes usage statistics, crash reports and shared store data on our behalf. See Firebase privacy information.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Usage statistics and crash reports may be processed by Google outside Indonesia. Shared store data is stored in Indonesia.

7. How long we keep it

  • Usage statistics: up to 14 months. Detailed raw event exports: up to 60 days.
  • Crash reports: about 90 days.
  • Shared store data: while sharing is on; deleted when you stop sharing or ask us to delete it.
  • License activation records: while the license is active, and as long as needed for legal and accounting purposes.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Shared store data is protected by access rules so that each device can only access its own data. Data on your device is protected by your device's security, so keep your device locked and your backups safe.

9. Your rights

Under Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022), you can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw your consent, or object to its processing. You can withdraw consent to store-data sharing yourself in the App (section 4). For other requests, e-mail rsriervina@gmail.com and include your device identifier or store name; we will respond within the time required by law.

10. Device permissions

  • Camera: to scan barcodes and take photos of menu items.
  • Bluetooth: to connect to receipt printers and cash drawers.
  • Photos and files: to choose a store logo or menu photos, and to create, share and restore backups and reports.
  • Internet: for license activation, usage statistics, crash reports and, if you agree, store-data sharing.

11. Children

The App is a business tool intended for people aged 18 or older. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal data.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The new version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date. If a change affects what we collect or why, we will tell you in the App and, where required, ask for your consent again.

Statistik penggunaan, laporan crash, dan data toko yang dibagikan ditautkan ke pengenal perangkat: nilai hash satu arah yang dibuat dari informasi perangkat Anda. Nilai ini tidak mengungkap nomor telepon, nama, atau e-mail Anda. Kami menggunakannya untuk menghubungkan laporan crash atau permintaan bantuan ke perangkat yang tepat, dan untuk mengenali lisensi Pro di perangkat Anda. Firebase juga membuat pengenal acak tersendiri untuk aplikasi.

d. Aktivasi lisensi

Saat Anda mengaktifkan atau menonaktifkan lisensi Pro, Aplikasi mengirim kunci serial, pengenal perangkat, dan untuk penonaktifan, data yang Anda masukkan untuk menerima kode satu kali, ke server aktivasi kami.
